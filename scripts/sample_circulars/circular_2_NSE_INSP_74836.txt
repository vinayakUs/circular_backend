Sub: Modification and deletion of certain provisions of Exchange Circular Ref. No. NSE/COMP/50957 dated January 07, 2022 — illustrative list of activities under third party product distribution

Reference No: NSE/INSP/74836
Date: June 23, 2026
Department: INSPECTION

In partial modification of Exchange Circular Ref No. NSE/COMP/50957 dated January 07, 2022, regarding the illustrative list of activities under third party product distribution, the following changes are made with effect from June 23, 2026:

1. Point No. 1 and 2 of illustrative list of activities issued vide Exchange Circular Ref No. NSE/COMP/50957 dated January 07, 2022 are modified as below:

   Point 1 (modified): Issuing Corporate Guarantees towards credit facilities availed by any entity, including group companies.

   Point 2 (modified): Deposit pledged with the bank for the purpose of availing credit facilities by group companies.

2. Point No. 3 to 12 of illustrative list of activities as prescribed in Exchange Circular Ref No. NSE/COMP/50957 dated January 07, 2022, stand deleted.

3. Members may refer to Annexure-A of this circular for FAQs on the current framework.

Annexure-A

Question No.1: Whether members can offer third-party products to their clients.

Answer: Members can offer third-party products to their clients only if such products are regulated by an appropriate financial sector regulator. Members are advised to ensure due diligence before onboarding any third-party product.

Question No.2: Whether prior approval from the Exchange is required for offering a new third-party product.

Answer: Members are required to intimate the Exchange before launching any new third-party product. The Exchange reserves the right to seek additional information or raise objections.

Question No.3: Whether digital gold products can be distributed.

Answer: Members may distribute digital gold products only if the underlying product is regulated by an appropriate financial sector regulator. Members must comply with all applicable KYC and disclosure requirements.

Question No.4: Whether members can enter into revenue sharing arrangements with third-party product providers.

Answer: Members may enter into revenue sharing arrangements subject to the limits and conditions specified by SEBI from time to time.

Question No.5: Whether members can act as a sponsor or trustee for any collective investment scheme.

Answer: No. Members cannot act as a sponsor or trustee for any collective investment scheme without obtaining separate registration from SEBI.

Question No.6: Whether members can offer unregulated third-party products.

Answer: No. Members cannot offer third-party products that are not regulated by an appropriate financial sector regulator. Refer Exchange Circular NSE/INSP/68566 dated December 12, 2024 for the current framework.

For any clarification, kindly contact the Inspection Department.
