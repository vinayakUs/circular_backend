Sub: Illustrative list of activities not permitted under third party product distribution

Reference No: NSE/COMP/50957
Date: January 07, 2022
Department: COMPLIANCE

In terms of SEBI Circular SEBI/HO/MIRSD/CRADT/CIR/P/2020/53 dated October 08, 2020, members are advised that the following activities are NOT permitted under the framework for distribution of third party products:

1. Issuing Corporate Guarantees towards credit facilities availed by any entity, including group companies, where such credit facilities are not in connection with the member's own business activities.

2. Deposit pledged with the bank for the purpose of availing credit facilities by group companies.

3. Borrowing of funds for the purpose of onward lending to clients or group companies.

4. Lending of funds to clients or group companies against shares, debentures, or other securities.

5. Underwriting or sub-underwriting activities beyond the limits prescribed by SEBI.

6. Acting as a sponsor or trustee for any collective investment scheme.

7. Providing margin trading facilities beyond the limits specified by the Exchange.

8. Offering assured returns on any investment product.

9. Acting as a portfolio manager without obtaining separate registration from SEBI.

10. Distribution of unlisted securities to retail clients.

11. Acting as a referral agent for any financial product without a proper agreement.

12. Entering into any arrangement/scheme, including but not limited to digital gold, e-gold, sovereign gold bonds, or any other arrangement/scheme, for facilitating the clients to transact in third-party products which are not regulated by an appropriate financial sector regulator.

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive. Members are required to ensure compliance at all times.
